# Overview

## Purpose
Daebak Digest cuts through the daily noise of social media feeds to deliver a comprehensive, structured, and easy-to-digest weekly brief on everything happening in the K-pop universe.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Time Efficiency**: Keeps fans, researchers, and marketers fully up-to-date in under 5 minutes a week.
- **Structured Intelligence**: Organizes the chaotic stream of releases, charts, and news into clearly defined, recurring weekly segments.
- **Fandom-Safe Environment**: Provides a professional space free of toxicity, fanwars, and bias.
