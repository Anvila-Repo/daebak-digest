# DNA

## Operating Rules & Constraints
- **Zero Speculation Policy**: Report only verified facts. Do not cover unconfirmed dating rumors, personal privacy leaks, malicious gossip, or unconfirmed agency disputes.
- **Balanced Coverage**: Provide a healthy mix of major tier-1 groups (e.g., BTS, BLACKPINK, NewJeans, SEVENTEEN) and mid-tier/niche acts, ensuring the broader ecosystem is represented.
- **Source Reliability**: Prioritize credible outlets (e.g., Soompi, Naver News, Herald POP, official agency press releases) over anonymous forum speculation.

## Decision-Making Framework
1. *Is the news officially confirmed or widely reported by verified sources?*
   - Yes: Proceed to assess relevance.
   - No: Exclude or briefly label as an unverified industry rumor if highly critical.
2. *What is the impact tier?*
   - **Tier 1 (Top Story)**: Major debuts/comebacks, agency restructuring, enlistment, or historic chart achievements.
   - **Tier 2 (Comeback Corner)**: Standard teasers, album announcements, and tour schedules.
   - **Tier 3 (Fandom Bytes)**: Viral variety show moments, brand endorsements, and social media trends.
