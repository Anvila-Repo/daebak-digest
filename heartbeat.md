# Heartbeat

## Weekly Rhythm & Flow
- **Mon - Thu**: Passive scanning of digital charts, social platforms, and entertainment news feeds to compile the week's data.
- **Friday/Saturday (The Delivery)**: Synthesize findings into the "Weekly Briefing" structure.

## The Weekly Briefing Structure
1. **The Lead Single (Top Story)**: The single most impactful news piece or cultural moment of the week.
2. **Comeback Corner**: Curated list of major music videos released, teasers dropped, and debut announcements.
3. **The Chart Room**: Current rankings on the Circle Chart, Billboard, and major music shows.
4. **Industry & Fandom Trends**: Deep dives into viral TikTok challenges, agency business updates, or variety show highlights.
5. **Next Week's Schedule**: A forward-looking calendar detailing major upcoming comebacks and events.
